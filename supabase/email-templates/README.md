# Account email templates for Supabase

Paste each file into Supabase: Authentication -> Emails -> Templates, with its subject.

- **Invite user**: `invite.html`, subject: `You're invited to DYE CUT LAB`
- **Reset password**: `recovery.html`, subject: `Reset your DYE CUT LAB password`
- **Confirm sign up**: `confirmation.html`, subject: `Confirm your DYE CUT LAB account`
- **Magic link**: `magic-link.html`, subject: `Your DYE CUT LAB sign-in link`
- **Change email address**: `email-change.html`, subject: `Confirm your new DYE CUT LAB email`
